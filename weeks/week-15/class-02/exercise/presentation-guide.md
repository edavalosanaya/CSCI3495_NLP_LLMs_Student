# Final Presentations: Facilitation Guide

Presentations span **W15C2 (Session 1)** and the **finals period (Session 2)**.
Plan ~10 minutes per group (8 talk/demo + 2 Q&A + transition).

## Facilitator timeline (per session)
| Min | Step |
|-----|------|
| 0-3 | Norms: timing, all members present, audience fills feedback forms |
| 3-… | Talks back-to-back, ~10 min each (keep strict time; give a 1-min warning) |
| last 5 | Collect feedback forms; quick reflections; logistics for the final exam/report |

## Running it smoothly
- **Order:** post the schedule in advance; have the next group queued at the side.
- **Timekeeping:** hold up a 2-min and a 30-sec card. Cut off at the limit, fairness.
- **AV:** pre-load decks; have a USB/backup. Ask presenters to bring a **demo fallback**
  (screenshots/recording) so a failed live demo doesn't derail the talk.
- **Q&A:** if the room is quiet, the facilitator asks one substantive question.

## Presenter checklist (share with students beforehand)
- [ ] Talk fits in **5-8 minutes**; every member speaks.
- [ ] Slide 1 states the **problem in one sentence** and why it matters.
- [ ] **Method** is explained clearly enough to be reproduced.
- [ ] At least **one measured result** (a number), with the right baseline/metric.
- [ ] One **limitation** or honest negative result is named.
- [ ] Demo is **rehearsed** and runs in the course Docker image (or has a fallback).
- [ ] You can answer: "Why this metric? What would you do with more time?"

## Grading
The instructor grades with `project/RUBRICS.md` (final report + presentation = 20%).
Presentation-specific lines: clear story, all members present, demo/result quality.
**Rigor over scale**: small, careful, honest work is rewarded; negative results count.

## Audience norms (participation-graded)
- Complete a `peer-feedback-form.md` for **each** talk.
- Note **one specific strength** and **one genuine question** per group.
- Ask helpful, not "gotcha," questions. Celebrate honest negative results.

## After presentations
- Confirm **final report** due date/location (`project/final/report-template.md`).
- Confirm **paper final exam** time/place (covers the term; the required ethics
  reading, Stochastic Parrots, included , see `exams/`).
