# Where this data comes from

`eng-spa.tsv` is a filtered subset of the **Tatoeba** corpus, obtained
through **OPUS** (release v2023-04-12).

- Tatoeba: <https://tatoeba.org>
- OPUS: <https://opus.nlpl.eu/Tatoeba-v2023-04-12.php>
- Licence: **CC BY 2.0 FR** <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/>

The sentences were written and translated by Tatoeba volunteers. This
copy is lowercased, stripped of punctuation, and filtered to sentences
of at most four tokens drawn from the most frequent 1000 English and
1500 Spanish words. Accents are preserved.

If you use OPUS in your own work, cite:

> J. Tiedemann, 2012. *Parallel Data, Tools and Interfaces in OPUS.*
> In Proceedings of LREC 2012.
