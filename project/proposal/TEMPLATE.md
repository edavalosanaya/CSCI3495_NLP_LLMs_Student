## 1. Problem & motivation
What question are you answering, and why is it interesting/important? (3-5 sentences.)
Write it as an actual question, with a question mark.

## 2. Background
1-3 sentences on the most relevant prior work or paper you build on (cite it).

## 3. Plan
- **Approach/method:** what you'll do.
- **Data:** dataset(s) and size (keep it small/CPU-friendly).
- **Models/tools:** which Ollama/HF models; key libraries.
- **Evaluation:** how you'll measure success (metrics, baselines). "We'll see if it
  works" is not a metric: name the number and what you compare it against.

## 4. Milestones & risks
- What will be done by the Week 12 checkpoint vs. final.
- Biggest risk and your backup plan if the main idea doesn't pan out.
